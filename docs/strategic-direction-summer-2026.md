# RoofSignal Strategic Direction - Summer 2026

## Strategic Principle

Collect data today as if it may become valuable tomorrow.

Data that was never captured can never become intelligence later.

## Fundamental Business Insight

RoofSignal does not sell drone inspections.

RoofSignal sells:

- Maintenance prioritisation.
- Objective decision support.
- Portfolio insight.
- Risk reduction.
- Historical building intelligence.
- Independent reporting.

The drone is a data acquisition tool. It is not the proposition.

## Drone Positioning

The drone should act as proof, not as the product.

Correct positioning:

> The drone is the evidence source.

Incorrect positioning:

> RoofSignal is a drone company.

Visual and commercial balance:

- 70% outcomes and decisions.
- 20% evidence and findings.
- 10% drone presence.

## Hardware Strategy

Professional hardware can be rented when needed.

This lowers startup risk significantly for:

- Thermal drones.
- Enterprise drones.
- Specialised payloads.
- Higher-end camera systems.

The primary bottleneck is customer acquisition, not hardware ownership.

## Optical Zoom Requirement

Optical zoom is mandatory for serious inspection work.

Without optical zoom:

- Gutters cannot be inspected properly.
- Flashing and lead/zinc details become harder to judge.
- Chimney inspections become riskier.
- Pilots are pushed unnecessarily close to objects.

Future platform requirements:

- Optical zoom.
- Stable gimbal.
- Precise camera control.
- Enterprise workflow support.

## Standardisation

RoofSignal should not depend on individual pilot skill.

Goal:

> Two different pilots should produce nearly identical outputs.

This requires:

- Fixed workflows.
- Fixed inspection paths.
- Fixed report structures.
- Fixed outputs.
- Repeatable image capture standards.
- Minimum evidence requirements per building element.

## NEN 2767 Alignment

RoofSignal should work NEN 2767-aligned from day one.

This means every inspection workflow, report template and data model should capture defects per building element using:

- Seriousness.
- Extent.
- Intensity.
- Indicative condition score.
- Maintenance priority.
- Evidence and confidence.

Do not overclaim. A quickscan or object report may use NEN 2767-aligned condition thinking without being sold as a full certified NEN 2767 condition measurement. A full NEN 2767 product tier can be added once scope, training, QA and liability coverage support it.

Strategic rule:

> Competitors can claim the norm. RoofSignal should operationalise the norm as reusable structured data.

See also: `docs/nen-2767-operating-standard.md`.

## Full Dataset Capture

While on-site, capture the maximum useful dataset whenever operationally reasonable.

Examples:

- Roof imagery.
- Facade imagery.
- Chimney imagery.
- Gutters.
- Details.
- Contextual photographs.
- GPS positions.
- Structured metadata.

The customer receives the deliverables associated with their package.

RoofSignal should retain the complete internal dataset when contractually and legally allowed.

Advantages:

- Later upsell.
- Additional modules.
- Historical comparison.
- Internal learning.
- AI training data where rights allow it.
- Future products.

Key operating phrase:

> RoofSignal sells reports but builds datasets.

## Autonomous Inspection Vision

Do not build this first, but preserve the path.

Phases:

1. Manual drone flight.
2. Waypoint-assisted inspection.
3. Automatic 3D building reconstruction.
4. Autonomous inspection routes.
5. Historical comparison and predictive maintenance.

Desired future workflow:

1. Drone scans building geometry.
2. 3D model is generated.
3. Inspection route is automatically generated.
4. Drone performs standardised inspection.
5. Findings are compared against previous inspections.

Benefits:

- Lower pilot dependency.
- Lower operational risk.
- Higher consistency.
- Better scalability.
- Stronger historical analysis.

## Building Intelligence Vision

RoofSignal is evolving from:

> Drone inspection company

towards:

> Building intelligence platform

Long-term mental model:

> Carfax for buildings.

This is not a 2026 product promise. It is a strategic direction.

All decisions today should preserve the option of reaching that future.

## Pilot Market: De Parken Apeldoorn

Residents of De Parken in Apeldoorn are a useful pilot market, but not a long-term B2C strategy.

Purpose:

- Process validation.
- Report validation.
- Flight experience.
- Case study generation.
- Testimonial generation.
- Local network building.
- Trust building.
- Warm introductions into larger B2B organisations.

Strategic rationale:

- Many residents are business owners, directors, CEOs, senior managers, investors, board members or real estate owners.
- They may influence company real estate, investment properties, VvE boards, commercial buildings, industrial buildings or property portfolios.
- Every residential inspection may therefore become a B2B lead.

Positioning:

- Do not position RoofSignal as aerial photography or drone inspection.
- Position RoofSignal as independent building intelligence, objective maintenance insight, preventive maintenance, condition assessment and maintenance prioritisation.

Key message:

> Geen aannemer. Geen herstelverkoop. Wel objectief inzicht.

Offer:

- RoofSignal Woningscan.
- Roof inspection.
- Facade inspection.
- Gutters.
- Chimney.
- Visible defects.
- Annotated photographs.
- Prioritised findings.
- Maintenance recommendations.
- Executive summary, priority list, photographic evidence and maintenance horizon.
- Indicative price: EUR 395 - EUR 495.

Success criteria:

- First reports.
- First testimonials.
- First references.
- First B2B introductions.
- First repeat customers.

The objective is not 100 residential customers. Residential customers are high-trust B2B lead generation.

## Decision Filter

When choosing what to build or sell next, prefer actions that:

- Win the next customer.
- Improve the next report.
- Increase repeatability.
- Capture structured data.
- Preserve future intelligence value.
- Avoid making RoofSignal look like a generic drone company.
